Privacy Policy

This page explains what data this website does and does not handle. The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising scripts and no cookies set by us.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels, social embeds or third-party scripts. There is no contact form, newsletter or login.

Local storage

The puzzle stores your best completion time for each board size in your browser’s localStorage. It never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us. Clearing browser storage deletes it.

Server logs

The site is served by Vercel, a static hosting provider. Like any web server, Vercel may process standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We do not receive or analyse that data in this project. See Vercel’s privacy policy for details.

External links

Pages link to the official app listing on Google Play. Once you follow an external link, that site’s privacy policy applies. We do not control it.

Children

The site is a general-audience guide and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this site ever adds analytics or another data-processing feature, this page will be updated first and the change date below will move.
