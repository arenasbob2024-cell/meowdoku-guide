About Meowdoku Guide

Meowdoku Guide is an independent fan project: a rules reference, a strategy guide and an original browser puzzle for a game we play. It is not the official app and has no connection to Oakever Games.

Who runs this site

The site is built and maintained by an independent developer as part of a small collection of free browser tools. We do not publish a personal name or a fabricated biography; the work is the site itself. If that changes, this page will say so.

Editorial policy

  • Rules and store facts are sourced. Game rules, platforms, price, rating and download figures are checked against the public Google Play listing on the date shown.
  • Our puzzles are ours. Every practice board is generated for this site and verified to have exactly one solution. No Oakever level layouts, artwork, branding or assets are copied.
  • Guides are written from the rules. Strategy pages explain deduction patterns; they do not reproduce the app’s tutorial text.
  • Corrections. If the app changes a rule or control, the app wins; we update the page.
  • No fake credentials. We do not claim to be the developer, an official partner or a professional puzzle organisation.

Intellectual property

“Meowdoku” and the official app belong to Oakever Games. We use the name only to describe and discuss the game, with a clear non-affiliation notice on every page. The game’s rules are functional mechanics; our expression of them — text, diagrams, code and generated puzzles — is original. We do not host, embed or redistribute the official app or its assets. If the owner has a concern about this fan site, we will respond and change or remove material as appropriate.

What we do not do

  • We do not provide the official app, APK files or mods.
  • We do not sell anything, run ads or collect accounts.
  • We do not publish Oakever’s daily puzzle answers.

Privacy

See the privacy page: no analytics, no cookies, no account, and only local browser storage for your best times.
